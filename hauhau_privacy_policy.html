Privacy Policy for HauHau

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HauHau ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the HauHau mobile application (the "App").

By creating an account or using HauHau, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a proximity-based matching and social experience for dog owners, HauHau collects the following types of data:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Authentication Data: HauHau allows users to create an account and log in using an E-mail address and password or via Google Sign-In (Google Account). When you register or log in, HauHau collects your unique user identifier (UID), email address, and basic profile display name provided by Google (if you choose Google Sign-In).
  • Dog Profile Data: Information you voluntarily enter about your dog, including your dog's name, breed, gender, birth year, biography (up to 400 characters), personality tags, and activity interests.
  • User-Generated Content: Photos of your dog that you upload to your profile, as well as swipe interactions and chat messages exchanged with matched users.

B. Location Information

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS): With your permission, HauHau collects your device's latitude and longitude coordinates (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION).
  • Derived Location: HauHau converts your coordinates into a City, Country, and Geohash to calculate approximate distances (e.g., "1.5 km away") between you and other nearby users. HauHau does not display your exact GPS pin or home address to other users.

C. Device & Usage Information

  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, app crash logs, and basic diagnostics used to maintain app stability and security.

2. How HauHau Uses Your Information

HauHau uses the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Core App Functionality: To create your account, display your dog's profile to nearby users, calculate distances between users, and facilitate matches and chats.
  • Authentication & Security: To verify your identity via E-mail or Google Account, prevent spam or fraudulent accounts, and enforce our community standards.
  • App Improvement: To diagnose technical issues, fix crashes, and improve overall performance.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

HauHau does not sell your personal data to third parties. However, HauHau relies on trusted third-party service providers (SDKs) to operate:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage): Used for secure user login (E-mail and Google Sign-In), database hosting, and storing uploaded dog photos.
  • Google Sign-In: Used if you choose to authenticate using your Google Account.

These services process your data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and strict security standards. HauHau may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

HauHau retains your personal data and dog profile for as long as your account remains active.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

In compliance with Google Play policies and GDPR, you have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Delete Account inside the HauHau app to permanently remove your profile, uploaded photos, and match history.
  2. Email Request: You may also request complete account and data deletion by emailing OneManFoundry@protonmail.com with the subject line "HauHau Account Deletion Request". HauHau will process your request and permanently erase your data within 30 days.

5. Data Security

HauHau values your trust and uses commercially acceptable means—including Firebase encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and Firebase Security Rules—to protect your personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

HauHau is intended for users aged 16 and older (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). HauHau does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If HauHau discovers that a child has provided personal data, it will be deleted immediately.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & Privacy Laws)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area), you have the right to access, rectify, export, or erase the personal data HauHau holds about you, as well as the right to withdraw location permissions at any time via your Android device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

HauHau may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
